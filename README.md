# Raritone Summer Internship 2026

## Internship Overview

This repository contains all tasks, research work, reports, documentation, presentations, and deliverables completed during the Raritone Summer Internship 2026.

The internship focused on exploring Artificial Intelligence applications in the fashion industry, particularly in areas such as Virtual Try-On Systems, Fashion Recommendation Engines, Computer Vision, Human Pose Estimation, Human Parsing, Segmentation, and AR/VR-based fashion experiences.

The objective of the internship was to study modern fashion technologies, understand their workflows, analyze available datasets, explore existing AI models, and document the findings through reports, presentations, and technical documentation.

---

## Internship Objectives

- Study AI applications in fashion technology.
- Research 2D and 3D Virtual Try-On systems.
- Explore Human Pose Estimation and Body Landmark Detection.
- Analyze Segmentation and Human Parsing techniques.
- Investigate Fashion Recommendation Systems.
- Examine datasets used in fashion AI research.
- Understand AR and VR integration in fashion platforms.
- Document findings and research outcomes.
